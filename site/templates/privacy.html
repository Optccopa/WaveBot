Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-01

1. Scope

This policy describes what data WaveBot collects, how it is used, and how long it is kept. By using the Bot or web panel, you consent to the practices described here.

2. Data collected

  • Guild metadata: guild ID, name, icon, member count, owner ID. Stored to render the panel and to compute partner eligibility.
  • Configuration: partner channel, ad channel, log channel, member role, and partner ping role IDs you set via /config set, /setup, or the web panel.
  • Ad text: the most recent message in your configured ad channel. Cached so it can be re-sent during partner rounds.
  • Moderation results: hash of your ad and the automated scan result (suspicious / not suspicious) returned by the moderation system.
  • Partnership records: when two servers are partnered, a record is kept of both guild IDs, guild names, member counts at the time of matching, the ping type used on each side, and a timestamp. This powers the "Partner Info" button on partner ads.
  • Premium status: guild ID, the ping types granted, the Discord user ID of the operator who granted it, and the grant date.
  • Reports: if you report a partner server, we store your Discord user ID, the server you reported from, the reported server, your chosen category, and your written answers. Reports are forwarded to a staff-only channel for review.
  • Operator actions: the Discord user ID of the moderator who added a blacklist entry, whitelisted link, or early-access grant, plus the reason.
  • Operational logs: routine bot activity (commands and buttons used, including the username and server where they were used; matching decisions; errors) is sent to private, staff-only log channels for debugging and abuse prevention.
  • Flagged ads: if the automated moderation system flags your ad, the ad text and your server's ID are forwarded to a staff-only review channel in the support server.
  • Web sessions: an encrypted session cookie containing your Discord user ID, username, avatar, and a CSRF token. No password is stored — login uses Discord OAuth2.

The Bot does not log message content outside your configured ad channel. It does not track DMs, voice activity, or messages.

3. Storage

  • Postgres: guild configuration, blacklist, whitelisted links, early-access grants, premium status, partnership records, reports. Persistent until the row is deleted.
  • Redis: short-lived caches (configs, ad text, scan results, partner cooldowns, partner statistics, OAuth state, rate-limit counters). Most keys expire automatically; see retention below.

4. Third parties

  • Discord: the Bot interacts with Discord's API. Discord's own privacy policy applies to data handled on their platform.
  • Anthropic: ad text is sent to Anthropic's Claude API for automated content moderation. See Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • Hostinger: the Bot and website are hosted on Hostinger's VPS infrastructure. See Hostinger's privacy policy.

No data is sold or shared with third parties for advertising.

5. Retention

  • Cached configs: up to 30 days, refreshed on read.
  • Cached ad text: until the ad channel updates or the Bot is removed from the guild.
  • Scan results: 30 days.
  • Partner cooldowns: 2 days.
  • Early-access records (guild ID, granting user ID, reason): until removed by an operator.
  • Premium records: until removed by an operator.
  • Partnership records: retained indefinitely so the "Partner Info" button on past partner ads keeps working; deleted on request.
  • Reports: retained until actioned and then kept for moderation history; deleted on request.
  • Operational logs: retained in staff-only Discord channels subject to Discord's own retention.
  • Web session cookies: until logout or cookie expiry.
  • Postgres records: until you remove the Bot or request deletion.

6. Removal

Removing the Bot from your server clears the live caches for that guild (cached config, ad text, premium status). To request full deletion of persistent rows (guild config, partnership records, blacklist appeal records), contact the operators via the support server.

7. Security

Sessions use encrypted cookies. The internal validation API is not exposed publicly. We do not, however, guarantee absolute security; do not store secrets in your ad message or configuration.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated at any time. Material changes will be announced in the support server.

9. Contact

Privacy questions or deletion requests: support server.
