Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-07

1. Scope

This policy describes what data WaveBot collects, how it is used, and how long it is kept. By using the Bot or web panel, you consent to the practices described here.

2. Data collected

  • Guild metadata: guild ID, name, icon, member count, owner ID. Stored to render the panel and to compute partner eligibility.
  • Configuration: partner channel, ad channel, log channel, member role, and partner ping role IDs you set via /config set, /setup, or the web panel.
  • Ad text: the most recent message in your configured ad channel. Cached so it can be re-sent during partner rounds.
  • Moderation results: hash of your ad and the automated scan result (suspicious / not suspicious) returned by the moderation system.
  • Operator actions: the Discord user ID of the moderator who added a blacklist entry or whitelisted link, plus the reason.
  • Web sessions: an encrypted session cookie containing your Discord user ID, username, avatar, and a CSRF token. No password is stored — login uses Discord OAuth2.

The Bot does not log message content outside your configured ad channel. It does not track DMs, voice activity, or user behaviour.

3. Storage

  • Postgres: guild configuration, blacklist, whitelisted links. Persistent until the row is deleted.
  • Redis: short-lived caches (configs, ad text, scan results, partner cooldowns, OAuth state, rate-limit counters). Most keys expire automatically; see retention below.

4. Third parties

  • Discord: the Bot interacts with Discord's API. Discord's own privacy policy applies to data handled on their platform.
  • Anthropic: ad text is sent to Anthropic's Claude API for automated content moderation. See Anthropic's privacy policy.

No data is sold or shared with third parties for advertising.

5. Retention

  • Cached configs: up to 30 days, refreshed on read.
  • Cached ad text: until the ad channel updates or the Bot is removed from the guild.
  • Scan results: 30 days.
  • Partner cooldowns: 3 days.
  • Web session cookies: until logout or cookie expiry.
  • Postgres records: until you remove the Bot or request deletion.

6. Removal

Removing the Bot from your server clears the live caches for that guild. To request full deletion of persistent rows (guild config, blacklist appeal records), contact the operators via the support server.

7. Security

Sessions use encrypted cookies. The internal validation API is not exposed publicly. We do not, however, guarantee absolute security; do not store secrets in your ad message or configuration.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated at any time. Material changes will be announced in the support server.

9. Contact

Privacy questions or deletion requests: support server.
